Current Champion of the UEFA Champions League

Team: Manchester City

Manchester City is the current champion of the UEFA Champions League. Below is a brief overview of their journey to victory:

Group Stage

Manchester City performed exceptionally well in the group stage, securing their spot in the knockout stage.

Knockout Stage

In the knockout stage, Manchester City faced several formidable opponents:

  • They advanced through the Round of 16 with convincing wins against RB Leipzig.
  • In the Quarter-finals, they had a tough battle against Bayern Munich.
  • The Semi-finals featured intense matches against Real Madrid.
  • Finally, they triumphed in the thrilling Final against Inter Milan.

Key Players

Some of the key players who contributed to Manchester City's victory include:

  • Kevin De Bruyne
  • Riyad Mahrez
  • Phil Foden
