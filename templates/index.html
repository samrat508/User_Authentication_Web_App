UEFA Champions League

Overview

The UEFA Champions League is an annual club football competition organized by the Union of European Football Associations (UEFA).

Format

The tournament consists of several stages:

Group Stage

32 teams are divided into eight groups of four teams each. Each team plays a round-robin tournament, and the top two from each group advance.

Knockout Stage

16 teams move on to the knockout stage, which includes the Round of 16, Quarter-finals, Semi-finals, and the Final.

Trophies

  • Winning the UEFA Champions League is one of the most prestigious honors in European club football.
  • The winning team is awarded the UEFA Champions League Trophy.

Notable Winners

  1. Real Madrid - 13 titles
  2. AC Milan - 7 titles
  3. FC Barcelona - 5 titles
  4. Liverpool - 6 titles
  5. Bayern Munich - 6 titles
